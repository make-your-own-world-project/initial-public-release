# A Shared Language

![The recurring concepts of the architecture arranged around one intact local archive](assets/architecture-glossary.png)

**Artifact**: Any preserved input or generated product.

**Deposit**: An artifact entering the authoritative corpus. Its existence has value
even when no reason or relationship is yet known.

**Withdrawal**: A bounded retrieval or generated product made from selected deposits
and derived observations. It never replaces its sources.

**Representation**: A derived view such as extracted text, media description,
embedding, semantic unit, or classification.

**Observation event**: Evidence that a perspective, reaction, question, or report
occurred. It is distinct from the truth of any proposition inside it.

**Epistemic role**: The status of content as observation, claim, interpretation,
hypothesis, metaphor, feeling, question, plan, failure, correction, quotation, or
unknown.

**Temporal map**: The versioned relationship layer above the corpus, including
observation, validity, and supersession history.

**Personal Meaning Matrix**: A request-scoped, multi-dimensional projection of
source-bound significance contributions and uncertainties. It is not a personality
model.

**Backward walk**: Traversal from a context-rich target toward earlier related
evidence without assigning the final product structure.

**Forward reconstruction**: Reversal over the same selected graph to explain the
journey in a human-decodable order.

**Collapse**: Product-specific, explicitly lossy partitioning of selected graph
branches against semantic and rhetorical jobs.

**Cog or specialist**: A bounded component with declared inputs, outputs, limits,
cost, and authority.

**Assembly line**: The product-specific arrangement of specialists used to create a
withdrawal.

**Handoff**: The typed payload and responsibility transfer between specialists.

**Compact working model**: The portable evidence, reasoning, product requirements,
and expansion handles assembled for one output. It is not a trained general model.

**Payload ledger**: The audit record of what each stage received, produced, omitted,
cost, and contributed.

**Human Protocol**: The bidirectional boundary for adapting machine-held structure
to a particular human communication state without claiming access to the person's
internal implementation.

**Weave**: The measured trajectory and balance of delivery functions across a
product.

**Tightness**: A scale or density parameter applied to a weave without redefining
its delivery jobs.

**Grounding**: The requirement that a generated contribution remain traceable to
authorized evidence or be clearly typed as interpretation, uncertainty, or absence.

**Receipt gate**: Independent verification that required invariants held for exact
artifact bytes. A process exit alone is not acceptance.

**Supersession**: A later observation replacing an earlier interpretation for later
use without deleting the earlier historical state.

**Personal sovereignty**: The ownership and authority boundary around a person's
experience, identity, attention, expression, and capacity to determine meaning.

**Bounded external contribution**: An authorized, request-specific handoff in which
an outside specialist receives only the payload needed for one declared operation.
The payload can be useful while remaining insufficient to reconstruct the maintained
corpus, provenance, temporal history, personal meaning, or machinery that produces
future withdrawals. This boundary prevents a narrow contribution from becoming the
extraction and destructive reduction of human knowledge into institutional value.
