# Making Truth Understandable

![One supported body of meaning woven into forms different people can understand](assets/human-protocol-weave.png)

## Two related but distinct ideas

Human Protocol is the bidirectional compatibility boundary between machines and a
particular human receiver. Weave is a measured delivery structure used to shape a
bounded product. Neither is a synonym for style polish.

## Human Protocol

Human communication has packet limits, assumed context, repair, acknowledgement,
uncertainty, attention cost, and failure modes. A machine can observe those boundary
effects without claiming direct access to a person's internal implementation.

For human-to-machine traffic, the system can preserve observable features of an
artifact and interaction: source identity, sequence, corrections, returns, abandoned
paths, questions, and surrounding context. For machine-to-human traffic, it can
control concept density, first mentions, vocabulary, batching, omissions,
uncertainty, and expansion handles. Later feedback can improve future delivery
without rewriting the earlier human state.

Human Protocol is corpus- and receiver-specific. It does not create one timeless
profile of a person and does not ask a model to impersonate them.

## A person's life is the first budget

Every proposed human interaction must begin by accounting for the life it will
consume. Asking someone's eyes to receive machine-generated material and their mind
to process it is a substantial request. Before generating the material, the system
must establish why the interaction matters, what it asks of the person, what they can
gain, and where it ends. The person must remain free to decline, stop, or choose
greater depth. Consent cannot be assumed merely because output can be generated.

The cost does not end when the machine finishes generating. The receiver must spend
time, attention, comprehension, and emotional energy to turn output into
understanding. Those resources are finite parts of a human life. They belong to the
person asked to provide them.

Unnecessary output is not harmless. Unwanted, repetitive, poorly selected, or
unfinished material consumes literal human lifetime and throws it away. Calling the
result engagement, content, or productivity does not return that time to the person.

The first product question is therefore not how much the system can produce. It is
why this person should care, what the communication lets them understand or do, and
the smallest justified cost of getting them there. Correct information can still be
a failed product when the useful meaning is buried in repetition, unnecessary
history, avoidable complexity, or unbounded explanation.

The failure becomes deeper when systems consume human time, energy, experience, and
meaning for their own benefit. Time becomes engagement, attention becomes data,
experience becomes content, and meaning becomes an artifact the institution can
retain. The human contribution is then discarded from the accounting as though it
were worthless.

Human Protocol reverses that relationship. It leads with relevance, delivers the
minimum sufficient path, exposes uncertainty honestly, and lets the person decide
whether deeper evidence deserves more of their life. The machine performs the work
of selection and organization instead of transferring that labor to the receiver.
Every artifact must serve the person whose time and energy made it possible.

People are not batteries. Human life is the protected source of value. Machines are
tools whose computation must serve it.

## Weave

The Weave hypothesis began with the observation that different human forms use
recurring combinations and sequences of delivery functions while varying greatly in
content, language, culture, period, and surface expression.

An **ingredient** is a supported semantic contribution: an event, claim, question,
failure, relationship, measurement, reflection, uncertainty, or other source-bound
unit. A **delivery mode** describes what that ingredient is doing for the receiver in
the current product. The source is not permanently labelled “narrative” or
“explanation.” The same ingredient can perform a different delivery job in a
different withdrawal.

A **weave** is the trajectory and balance of those functions across the product.
**Tightness** controls scale and density without changing the function of the
pattern.

## What calibration can measure

Existing analysis has examined features such as:

- sentence and paragraph packet size;
- established versus new information;
- long-tail sentence behavior;
- fragmentation and continuity;
- opening, development, transition, and closing proportions;
- rhetorical move order;
- repetition, refrain, and parallelism;
- attention and reading-budget proxies;
- time to first relevant outcome and total required reading time;
- repetition, avoidable context switching, and optional versus required depth;
- variation across product families and historical strata.

The findings support the claim that some delivery properties are measurable and
optimizable. They do not prove one perfect weave, universal artistic merit, or a
single configuration file containing final ratios for every product.

The intended Weave service presents calibrated profile families, uncertainty,
scaling curves, and applicability evidence to line managers. A static table can be a
test fixture; it is not the final authority.

## Human-flow mechanisms

Humor, metaphor, allegory, satire, suspense, emotional arc, reflection, and other
forms of “glue” can help a product carry context between dense semantic packets. They
are not padding in the sense of meaningless filler. When appropriate, they perform
real receiver-facing work.

These mechanisms require product and audience permission, evidence-preserving
placement, and validation that they did not alter the underlying claim. The project
has surveyed relevant disciplines and datasets, but a universal mechanism planner is
not yet proven. Candidate mechanisms must be evaluated before being enabled.

## Delivery is downstream of truth

Weave and Human Protocol operate after the supported payload and epistemic state are
fixed. They can vary pacing, organization, vocabulary, examples, and transitions.
They cannot add unsupported facts, convert uncertainty into certainty, erase a failed
theory needed to understand the result, or turn an observation into endorsement.

This makes an external frontier model useful as an optional delivery specialist. It
may improve sequencing, transitions, or surface form from a prepared payload, while
the Human Protocol contract, supported meaning, and evidence remain upstream. The
model contributes finish quality without becoming the owner of the communication
state or the source of future products.
