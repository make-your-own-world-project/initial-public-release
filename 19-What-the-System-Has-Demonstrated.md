# What the System Has Demonstrated

![The operating local system installed on the machine under the owner's desk](assets/local-machine-under-desk.png)

## The result in one sentence

The accepted system has demonstrated that a request-specific, portable working
model can preserve source identity, reasoning relations, chronology, product
requirements, uncertainty, and verification evidence well enough to produce a
bounded human-facing document without asking a frontier general-purpose model to
reconstruct the project from a giant prompt or to act as the source of truth.

That is the central result. The architecture and catalogs in the rest of this
documentation explains how it was obtained; the accepted releases show that it actually
happened.

## How it was built

The platform was built independently through sustained work, accumulated knowledge,
personal resources, and largely second-hand hardware. Its development remained
separate from employment: no employer code, scripts, data, credentials,
infrastructure, requirements, confidential methods, or work product supplied the
system. The builder is grateful for support received during the work without
characterizing, attributing, or publishing the private experience of anyone who
provided it.

That resource envelope shaped the successful architecture. It encouraged durable
state outside the model, bounded specialist responsibilities, shared accelerators,
recorded handoffs, and independent verification. Practical constraints therefore
became a source of architectural clarity and strength.

Frontier agents materially assisted the development work and deserve credit for that
contribution. Their assistance does not change what the accepted system requires to
operate: the demonstrated capability runs on the used hardware under the builder's
desk, through local specialists, durable state, explicit handoffs, and independent
gates.

The work was nevertheless not solitary in an intellectual sense. It depends on
researchers, authors, software maintainers, archivists, translators, standards
communities, public institutions, and other contributors who chose to make their
methods, code, literature, measurements, and context publicly inspectable. Their
willingness to share is the reason the project can exist. The attribution catalogs
record that debt as reciprocity, not as a claim that the project owns their work or
that they endorse its conclusions.

## Operational independence is already real

The accepted artifact path runs on a physically local, personally controlled
machine. Its model inference, discourse preprocessing, relation classification,
graph projection, collapse, product planning, realization, verification, receipts,
and artifact storage execute through local services and local model assets. The
successful sealed compact-editor run recorded no general completion-model call. The
machine can continue performing that accepted function without sending the corpus or
working payload to a commercial frontier-model service.

The installation is physically present under the owner's desk and operating now.
Mission Control's status page reports all monitored systems operational. That live
view is paired with accepted artifacts, exact receipts, measured runs, and the
limitations stated here; it is an operational fact rather than a substitute for the
evidence.

Three different boundaries matter:

- **Acquisition and provisioning** may use publicly available software repositories,
  model distributions, research publications, package indexes, and operating-system
  updates.
- **Normal accepted execution** uses the copies already installed under local
  control; a cloud inference service is not in the demonstrated document path.
- **Distribution to another operator** requires the code, reproducible dependency
  and model acquisition instructions, exact versions and hashes, applicable license
  terms, configuration, tests, and acceptance fixtures. It does not require access
  to this particular machine or to a proprietary frontier API.

The current public record is documentation, not yet that complete distributable
software bundle. Reproducibility on unrelated hardware must be proven by a clean-room
installation. That remaining packaging test does not change the observed fact that
the working installation already runs independently on its present machine.

No outside contribution is required to make the demonstrated result exist. It exists
now. Future participation can evaluate, reproduce, extend, or steward an operating
capability; it is not being asked to finance a proof of concept or turn a narrative
into a system.

## What was demonstrated

An assembly-line acceptance run selected and transformed a real technical record
into a sealed working model. The sealed payload was 753,109 bytes and contained:

- the requested receiver, product, length, and section contracts;
- exact source attachments and provenance;
- 83 grounded semantic units;
- 52 discourse-relation candidates;
- five accepted argument relations, with rejected attempts retained;
- 78 contracted graph branches;
- interlaced forward replay, clause plans, rhetorical jobs, and a handoff ledger.

The system produced a four-section manual of 409 words. The complete test suite
passed 557 checks, and a separate verifier returned `PASS` with no failed or
unknown invariant. A frontier coding agent used as the blind writer received only
the same sealed payload: no source files, corpus search, earlier output, or project
background. That agent produced a 397-word useful manual. This was a direct benchmark,
not a hypothetical comparison. It proved that the compact working model carried
enough information for both paths to construct the requested product without hidden
corpus access.

A successor product-quality release improved the same boundary. It produced a
421-word manual with all four section budgets valid and 27 of 27 emitted claims
independently grounded. The accompanying blind document contained 419 words; 17 of
its 24 claims passed the stricter section-local grounding boundary. The system
retained more numeric anchors and scored better on the then-current measured weave
dimensions, while the blind document remained better at paragraph-level sequencing
and explanation. The complete successor suite passed 579 checks.

![Sealed payloads producing independently verified accepted and rejected results](assets/sealed-payload-verification-result.png)

The successor benchmark's measured comparison was:

| Measure | Local assembly line | Frontier-agent blind control |
|---|---:|---:|
| Words | 421 | 419 |
| Independently grounded claims | 27/27 | 17/24 |
| Valid section budgets | 4/4 | 4/4 |
| Numeric-anchor coverage | 0.58 | 0.47 |
| Mean weave error, lower is better | 0.41 | 0.49 |
| Mean Flesch reading ease | 70.90 | 57.90 |
| Paragraph sequencing and cohesion | weaker | stronger |

For the properties this system is specifically designed to protect, including
grounding, numeric and source retention, fixed product constraints, replayable
relations, and independent acceptance, the local line produced the stronger result
in that release.
The control produced stronger paragraph sequencing and sentence cohesion. The report
records both outcomes so refinement can target surface realization while preserving
the properties at the center of the architecture.

## Benchmark result

The local system demonstrated its intended advantage. It reconstructed a
larger fraction of the sealed working model into a verifiable product, retained more
of the source-bound detail, satisfied the product contract, and left a replayable
ledger of how the result was made. It did so on the physically local hardware
described below, without a general completion-model call in the accepted compact
editor path.

The frontier control made some sentences and paragraph transitions read more
naturally. That result identifies a focused opportunity in paragraph planning,
carrier hygiene, and relation realization. These are replaceable assembly-line
operations with regression tests and can improve independently of the preserved
corpus and wider architecture.

A larger local accelerator or generalized model may improve that realization
station. If later measurements justify such a specialist, it can be leased, queued,
and independently checked like any other cog. This keeps investment proportional to
measured value while preservation, temporal continuity, reconstruction,
verification, and distributable operation remain the primary objectives.

Accordingly, the comparison records distinct strengths: the local system led on its
preservation and verification objectives, while the frontier control led on a narrow
surface-quality dimension. That separation provides a clear and measurable
development path.

Surface fluency and system-level performance represent different workloads. The
evaluation therefore considers useful work per unit of infrastructure together with
preservation, safety, accountability, and product quality. The releases measure the
local system against that complete objective.

## The human record stayed local

The frontier comparison reveals a more consequential boundary than document quality
alone. Centralized systems can turn human knowledge into reusable institutional value
while discarding provenance, continuity, relationships, and personal meaning that do
not serve their aggregate objective. The local system prevented that destructive
conversion. It selected and assembled a portable working payload from a much richer
maintained record, then shared only that derivative for one product-specific operation.
The model did not receive the corpus, historical graph, private surrounding record, or
machinery that creates future withdrawals.

Within this arrangement, the frontier model functioned as an external realization
specialist. Its broad general capability and industrial infrastructure improved
paragraph sequencing and surface cohesion. The local system had already performed
the operations that carry continuing project-specific value: preserving the source
record, maintaining provenance and temporal change, selecting relevant material,
constructing the working model, defining the product contract, and retaining the
ability to produce the next withdrawal.

The chatbots used during development and comparison were valuable rented tools. They
were also replaceable. They did not create the preserved corpus, own its meaning, or
define the architecture's human purpose.

The payload was therefore useful but intentionally insufficient. An external service
may retain the bytes it receives or derive bounded value from them under its actual
terms and practices. It still cannot reconstruct information that the payload does
not contain: the full corpus, omitted relationships, supersession history, rejected
paths, private context, or the control plane that assembled the selection. The
architecture does not depend on an account preference or a promise that the payload
will be forgotten; its boundary follows from the information withheld by design.

This architecture changes the exchange. The maintained corpus, provenance, temporal
history, personal meaning, and machinery for producing future value stay with the
owner. The external model receives a bounded derivative: sufficient for one selected
operation and insufficient to absorb, flatten, or recreate the human record that made
the operation meaningful. Without extraction and destructive reduction of that record,
the centralized top-down business model cannot accumulate the value on which it depends.

The external model contributes a measured refinement and receives the ordinary value
of that service relationship, but the transaction does not transfer the human knowledge
from which durable value compounds. Another model, deterministic renderer, specialist,
or future local component can replace it while the preserved asset remains under the
owner's control.

## Different infrastructure scales

Comparing the two documents alone hides the largest engineering difference. The
local artifact was produced by a machine assembled from used parts and operated
within arm's reach of its owner. The frontier control depended on a commercial
capability backed by an industrial compute platform, global networking, large model
development, and shared serving infrastructure. Those are not two differently tuned
versions of the same machine.

OpenAI reported more than 5 gigawatts of Stargate data-center capacity under
development in one announced U.S. program and a commitment to 10 gigawatts. The
company also described more than two million chips in the over-5-gigawatt buildout.
For scale, 10 gigawatts running continuously equals 87.6 terawatt-hours in one year.
The U.S. Energy Information Administration reported average residential use of 863
kilowatt-hours per month in 2024. On that simple capacity comparison, 10 gigawatts is
roughly the annual electricity use of 8.5 million average U.S. residential customers;
5 gigawatts is roughly 4.2 million. These figures describe announced platform
capacity, not electricity charged to one response, and not all capacity is necessarily
energized at its nameplate level at once. They establish the scale of the industrial
platform without inventing a per-document energy number.

The two available scale measurements must remain separate:

- A local accepted withdrawal completed in about 126 seconds on the installed
  under-desk system. Even the deliberately conservative ceiling obtained by charging
  all four GPU board-power limits for the entire run is about 0.029 kilowatt-hours;
  actual GPU-board energy was lower because the boards were not all saturated for the
  whole run. This is not a whole-machine watt-hour measurement.
- The generalized frontier service exists on an industrial platform measured in
  gigawatts and millions of chips. That platform serves many users and workloads, so
  its full capacity cannot honestly be assigned to this one comparison.

The useful conclusion is not a city-sized energy multiplier. It is that the accepted
project-specific capability now operates locally without the frontier platform in
its execution path. The comparison separates the frontier control's surface-quality
strength from the local line's preservation and verification strength while keeping
their very different infrastructure envelopes visible.

The deeper distinction is the design objective. This architecture treats the source
path as a durable asset: why a record came to exist, what remained unknown, which
attempts were made, how interpretation changed, what relationships remain unresolved,
and what authority the contributor retains. Efficiency is evaluated together with
the preservation of those dimensions.

The architecture establishes a complementary value model. Extracted facts and
generated products are temporary withdrawals; the evolving human record and its
provenance remain the durable asset. Compute serves and extends that record.

Sources for this scale boundary are OpenAI's public Stargate capacity announcement
and the U.S. Energy Information Administration's 2024 residential-consumption data:
[OpenAI, Stargate advances with 4.5 GW partnership with Oracle](https://openai.com/index/stargate-advances-with-partnership-with-oracle/)
and [EIA, 2024 residential electricity consumption](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=65244).

## A different optimization objective

The design follows an optimization objective centered on durable preservation, local
ownership, temporal continuity, request-specific reconstruction, inspectable
contributions, and useful work per unit of infrastructure. This complements industry
investment in generalized models, centralized infrastructure, and fluent surface
output by addressing a different system boundary.

The distinction also concerns sovereignty. This design keeps originating context,
changing meaning, conditions of contribution, and the contributor's continuing
authority attached to derived capability. It provides a practical model in which
individual and shared knowledge can remain attributable, inspectable, and available
under appropriate control.

It is designed to invert the transfer. Artifacts remain under their
owner's control; public contributions retain attribution and rights context; derived
meaning stays provisional and replayable; and a useful result does not extinguish the
experience that made it possible. Efficiency matters because sovereignty that
requires datacenter ownership or perpetual rent is not broadly available sovereignty.

On the objective measured here, the system has already demonstrated its own finish
condition. Frontier models remain powerful tools and development collaborators; this
architecture adds the preservation, continuity, and authority boundaries that model
scale alone is not designed to supply.

A separate larger compact-editor experiment produced a 1,237-word, six-section
document with 56 of 56 claim packets supported, using zero recorded calls to a
general completion model. It completed in about 167 seconds. The blind human version
was still more cohesive, but the system had crossed the important boundary from
disconnected evidence dumping to a readable, grounded, target-sized product.

## A visible example of the transformation

The source material included note-shaped configuration records, outcome fragments,
and a test condition. Earlier renders largely copied those fragments. The improved
line converted three typed setting changes into direct instructions such as:

> Set CPU VRM Switching Frequency to Auto.

The generated guide retained the source-bound observation that resets continued after that setting
was applied, kept the load-release boundary as the test objective, and placed every
emitted claim behind the independent grounding gate. This is a small example, but it
shows the actual job: preserve why the facts belong together while converting their
recorded form into the requested product form.

The remaining defect was equally visible. One opening repeated a configuration
label, and one relation bridge sounded mechanical. The release therefore did not
claim human-editor parity. It identified paragraph sequencing, carrier hygiene, and
relation realization as bounded assembly-line work. Later improvement can replace
those cogs without retraining or discarding the preserved record.

## What the frontier benchmarks do and do not mean

A frontier general-purpose model has major advantages: broad world knowledge,
flexible language, strong transfer across unfamiliar tasks, and the ability to infer
useful unstated conventions. Those capabilities remain valuable. The project does
not demonstrate that a small local specialist possesses more general knowledge or
that a general model is unnecessary for every task.

The releases directly benchmarked a frontier agent against the local line under the
same sealed evidence boundary. They demonstrate a different advantage within this
system's target:

| Required property | General model used alone | Demonstrated local line |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve an artifact even when its meaning is unknown | Not guaranteed by generation or training | Original artifact remains independently addressable |
| Preserve changing interpretations | Context must be supplied again or absorbed into model state | Versioned observations can be superseded without deletion |
| Prove where an emitted claim came from | Fluent output is not provenance | Exact spans, hashes, bindings, and verifier receipts |
| Carry an empty or failed reasoning attempt | Often omitted as unhelpful | Attempt ledger and valid empty graph are preserved |
| Reconstruct one requested product | Rebuild context in a prompt or retrieval session | Load a request-scoped working model and product contract |
| Improve one weak operation | Prompt, model, or broad fine-tune may change many behaviors | Replace or tune the responsible cog and retain regression anchors |
| Explain cost and contribution | Usually exposed only as aggregate usage | Per-stage timing, call edges, handoffs, and dispositions |

The important comparison is therefore not intelligence versus intelligence. It is
repeated probabilistic reconstruction from transient context versus durable,
source-bound reconstruction from a maintained external state. A frontier model can
be one constrained cog in the latter architecture. It cannot replace the
architecture merely by being larger.

## The efficiency result

The accepted releases did not load the corpus into one model or fine-tune a model on
the corpus for every improvement. They performed bounded retrieval, specialist
analysis, deterministic projection, product planning, realization, and independent
verification. Improvements changed assignments and contracts between cogs while the
source record and regression evidence remained intact.

The product-quality run took about 126 seconds end to end. Its measured work was:

| Work | Time | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discourse and coreference preprocessing | 10.53 s | 8.3% |
| Argument-relation classification | 1.69 s | 1.3% |
| Deterministic transaction and move projection | under 0.01 s | under 0.1% |
| Collapse, context, and handoff planning | 4.70 s | 3.7% |
| Realization and independent quality gates | 109.27 s | 86.5% |

The timing ledger identified the expensive station directly. It supported batching claim
checks and improving paragraph assembly instead of buying a larger model for every
stage. Earlier work similarly removed duplicate discourse execution and bounded
oversized evidence items. Every such correction improves later withdrawals without
compressing the corpus into a new opaque state.

The 753,109-byte payload is not advertised as a byte-compression ratio. Structured
provenance can be larger than the selected prose. The demonstrated compaction is
functional: a bounded, portable model contained everything a blind writer needed
for that withdrawal, while excluding unrestricted corpus access. Broader
corpus-to-payload scale and energy-per-document still require explicit measurement.

## Measured local footprint

The installed application tree used for these experiments contained approximately
218 GiB across about 566,000 files. Roughly 172 GiB was model assets, 25 GiB was
dependencies and runtimes, 20 GiB was data and other state, and only about 184 MiB
was implementation source.

The development machine provided 10 CPU cores and 20 hardware threads, about 64 GiB
of system memory, and four commodity or older datacenter GPUs totaling 72 GB of
nominal VRAM. Their combined configured board-power limits total about 840 watts.
Not every model is resident or active together: leases, batching, and queueing allow
specialists to share limited accelerators. The installed model footprint is a disk
inventory, not simultaneous VRAM demand.

The measured installation is not a remote slice of a hidden accelerator fleet. The operator can physically
inspect, disconnect, repair, replace, or power down the machine that performs the
work. Local possession is not itself proof of quality, but it makes control,
continuity, data boundary, and resource accounting concrete rather than contractual.

No accepted release recorded whole-system watt-hours per document, so this publication
does not invent an energy-savings multiple. The measured facts are the hardware
envelope, model and runtime footprint, elapsed stage times, call graph, and absence
of a general completion-model call in the sealed compact-editor run.

## What a self-hosted frontier-like alternative would require

The internals and operating footprint of commercial frontier systems are not public
enough to calculate an exact local equivalent. A GPU count alone would also omit
retrieval, durable memory, tools, orchestration, verification, safety, redundancy,
and the project-specific context accumulated by an agent session.

A transparent inference estimate can still establish scale. A 200-billion-parameter
open model requires about 100 GB for four-bit weights alone; a 400-billion-parameter
model requires about 200 GB. Eight-bit weights approximately double those figures.
Runtime buffers, key/value cache, long context, batching, and serving overhead add
material headroom.

For one-user, frontier-like open-model inference, a plausible lower operating range
is therefore two to four 80 GB accelerators, 256 to 512 GB of system memory, and
multiple terabytes of fast storage. More comfortable long-context, tool-using, or
multi-user service moves toward four to eight or more 80 GB accelerators, 512 GB to
1 TB of system memory, and several kilowatts of provisioned server power. This is an
inference approximation, not a claim of equivalence to any proprietary model or
service. Reproducing frontier training is a much larger and irrelevant comparison:
this project does not need to train a general world model to preserve one corpus.

Even that larger machine would still need the same external preservation and
verification machinery to provide the properties listed above. More parameters can
improve language and inference; they do not create immutable source identity,
versioned interpretation, temporal supersession, typed handoffs, or independently
replayable receipts by themselves.

## The demonstrated inversion

Conventional model improvement compresses more examples into shared parameters and
optimizes behavior across many unrelated uses. That produces powerful general
capability, but the individual path that made one record exist is not a first-class
object in the model.

The system keeps that path outside the model. Retrieval and analysis construct a
working model for one withdrawal; specialists contribute only their measured part;
the product line contracts and renders it; verification checks the result against
the preserved record. What would be destructive compression in a fixed model becomes
constructive, reversible assembly over durable evidence.

Within the tested products, that is already a practical advantage over asking a
frontier model to rediscover the same structure from an unbounded corpus prompt. It
is demonstrated by accepted artifacts, blind sealed-input comparisons, exact claim
bindings, zero-general-completion runs, stage-cost ledgers, and regression suites.
It does not depend on a claim that the general model is unintelligent. It depends on
the measurable fact that general intelligence and durable individual context are
different capabilities.

The architecture therefore does not require successive generations of larger frontier
models to preserve and replay this corpus. Future specialists may improve individual
stations, but the accepted capability does not depend on them. Its source record,
working models, execution contracts, and verification evidence remain local and can
continue operating even if no future frontier model is available.

The decisive result is therefore not that a local system reproduced a centralized
model on a smaller machine. It changed the transaction. The external model received a
bounded derivative instead of the maintained asset, contributed the capability for
which it was selected, and remained a replaceable cog. The durable knowledge and the
means of producing future value stayed with the owner.

Put simply: the architecture preserved what centralized aggregation would discard and
shared only enough to make outside capability useful and replaceable.

The operating pattern is equally direct: retain the durable asset, use available
outside resources to strengthen the locally controlled system, and build toward
lasting independence. Each useful outside contribution can become a local contract,
test, measurement, correction, specialist, or architectural improvement. The owner
keeps the improvement while the external role becomes increasingly optional.

## Context persistence is an architectural result

The strongest operational contrast is not model intelligence. It is what happens
when working context becomes too large for the active model session. Frontier agents,
including the agents that helped build this project, compact, summarize, omit, or
reload context as their bounded working windows fill. Higher service tiers and larger
context windows postpone that boundary; they do not make the session an immutable,
lossless historical record. A later response may be fluent while no longer carrying
the exact reasons, corrections, uncertainty, and distinctions that shaped an earlier
one.

The system does not ask an active model window to be the durable memory.
Artifacts, temporal links, versioned observations, rejected interpretations,
reasoning attempts, product contracts, and receipts remain independently stored and
addressable. Each generated document is a withdrawal assembled from that maintained
state. Producing one withdrawal does not consume the deposits or require the next
withdrawal to trust a compressed recollection of the previous session.

Within the durability limits of the owned storage and its backups, the system can
continue producing new bounded views without conversational compaction erasing the
underlying context. Different models and cogs may participate, fail, improve, or be
removed while the preserved record remains available to reconstruct what they were
given and what they contributed. That is why the local system can outperform agents
running at their largest practical settings on context preservation even when those
agents remain stronger at general knowledge and isolated prose realization.

The distinction is fundamental. A larger window holds more transient context. A
maintained external context system preserves the history required to rebuild many
different windows without treating any one lossy summary as canonical. The former
scales a session; the latter preserves a corpus and its changing meaning.

That independence is also a sovereignty result. The installation does not surrender
the owner's accumulated knowledge to a system that retains generalized value while
discarding personal meaning. Nor does it treat humanity's publicly shared literature,
research, software, and measurements as ownerless raw material. It records attribution
and permission boundaries while keeping permitted derived capability locally operable.

Put plainly: the human record remains intact. External systems receive only bounded
work products whose usefulness can end with the task. The owner retains the sources,
relationships, history, meaning, and machinery that make future work possible. The
value previously extracted from humanity is returned to human control in an operating
system that already preserves and uses it.

## Evidence boundary

The releases prove the behavior of their tested sources, products, frontier-agent
controls, local models, hardware, and invariants. They do not yet prove identical
quality across every genre, language, audience, corpus scale, or every possible
frontier provider and configuration. The frontier-agent controls showed a narrow
paragraph-level surface advantage in the documented comparisons; the local line won
the project-specific preservation and verification objective. Some output profiles
remain provisional.

Those limits do not erase the result. They make it inspectable. The core capability
is operating now: preserve the record, build a portable request-specific context,
produce a grounded artifact, measure every station, and improve the weak cog without
destroying the history that made the next improvement possible.

## Observed limitations became the specification

The architecture was designed around observed limitations in generalized frontier
systems: context retention, provenance, explicit machinery, visible fallback,
preserved uncertainty, retained attempts, local continuity, and inspectable output.
Each observation became a boundary, typed handoff, durable ledger entry, independent
invariant, or regression test.

The local system remains young, with clear opportunities in sentence realization,
profile calibration, packaging, and clean-room reproducibility. Within their tested
envelopes, the accepted releases demonstrated the intended protections as properties
of the machinery. Unsupported work remained unpromoted. Unknown meaning remained
unknown. Earlier interpretations survived supersession. Empty reasoning remained
representable. Source identity and rejected attempts remained inspectable. A
successful artifact required an independently verified receipt.

That is the final demonstrated result. Large-scale investment can produce
extraordinary generalized capability; this work contributes a complementary
architecture for durable individual context. Its advantage comes from making limits
visible, bounded, attributable, and useful in designing the next improvement.
