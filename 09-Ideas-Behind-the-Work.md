# Ideas Behind the Work

![Scientific disciplines contributing bounded methods to a connected local architecture](assets/context-scientific-disciplines-hero.png)

## A multidisciplinary system

The architecture combines mechanisms from multiple disciplines. It does
not claim to have invented those fields or that the cited communities endorse this
implementation. Its contribution is the way their bounded capabilities are assigned,
measured, and connected around preservation of individual context.

## Major research lineages

### Provenance, temporal data, and graphs

Content addressing, append-only observation history, temporal validity,
supersession, typed graph relationships, and query-scoped projections supply the
preservation substrate. Graph algorithms can measure reach, centrality, bridging, and
branch cost, but those metrics remain separate from personal significance.

### Discourse and argumentation

Rhetorical Structure Theory supplies discourse relations and nuclearity structure.
Coreference methods supply candidate reference chains. Argument-mining work supplies
proposition-pair classification. xAIF and related argument interchange concepts
provide a useful graph vocabulary without becoming a second truth store.

The implementation has evaluated or used specialist machinery including IsaNLP RST,
FastCoref, AMF argument-relation classification, OpenVINO inference, BlingFire and
spaCy language processing, and NetworkX graph operations. Each tool remains bounded
to what its native output can support.

### Natural-language generation

The architecture follows the long-standing separation between content determination,
document planning, microplanning, and surface realization. Relevant traditions
include grammar-based realization, meaning-representation-to-text, graph-to-text,
lexically constrained decoding, and generate-then-reparse consistency checking.

The research has examined deterministic realization and compact specialist model
options. It does not treat a chatbot prompt as a substitute for a realizer contract.

### Rhetoric, narratology, and communication

Weave draws on rhetoric, discourse organization, narratology, genre studies,
linguistics, readability, information structure, and communication theory. The
project has compared long-term literary and publication patterns and treats cultural,
language, period, audience, and genre variation as evidence strata rather than noise.

Relevant conceptual families include rhetorical moves, given/new information,
coherence relations, narrative progression, analogy and structure mapping, humor and
figurative-language analysis, and audience-specific communication.

### Human factors

Human Protocol is informed by cognitive load, attention, packet size, repair,
acknowledgement, feedback, accessibility, human-computer interaction, and the
pragmatics of communication. Psychological and philosophical frameworks may provide
explicit lenses on appraisal, values, projects, constraints, affect, reflection, and
experience. Those lenses remain attributed and subjective; none is promoted into a
final theory of a person.

### Verification and software engineering

Typed contracts, capability manifests, immutable release identities, mutation tests,
independent verification, receipt gates, backpressure, bounded batching, idempotent
jobs, cancellation, rollback, and query-plan-style cost analysis provide the control
plane.

## Attribution as reciprocal architecture

These lineages exist here because researchers, authors, maintainers, translators,
archivists, institutions, and communities chose to make work available to others.
The resulting system therefore treats attribution as part of the same sovereignty rule it
applies to personal material. A useful idea does not become ownerless when it is
published.

The synthesis adds a bounded-participation pattern: a powerful external model can
serve one typed operation over an authorized payload without becoming the repository
of the corpus, the source of its relationships, or the authority for future
reconstruction. This is an architectural contribution, not a claim that the cited
communities endorse the implementation.

The accompanying attribution ledger records the contributor, source, permission
basis, bounded use, implementation status, and reciprocal return for the principal
works used in this public account. It distinguishes software that runs, methods that
were adapted, corpora that were measured, research that influenced design, and
experiments that were evaluated and rejected. None of those relationships implies
endorsement by the contributor.

## Source-use policy

Research and calibration prefer verified public-domain material, government
resources, and datasets whose licenses explicitly permit the intended analysis.
Sources requiring circumvention or uncertain authorization are excluded from new
ingestion unless separately reviewed.

Public-domain and openly licensed literature can contribute measured delivery
patterns. It does not become private training material hidden inside an untraceable
model. Source, license, transformation, measurement method, uncertainty, and intended
use remain in the attribution ledger.

## Completeness boundary

The private project contains a larger attribution inventory with additional papers,
works, code repositories, versions, evaluations, and rejected candidates. This public
publication includes a reviewed principal ledger without redistributing source text or
claiming that every explored mechanism is deployed. The ledger is deliberately
incomplete rather than falsely exhaustive; future scholarly releases should extend it
with reviewed bibliographies, license inventories, and reproducibility packages.
