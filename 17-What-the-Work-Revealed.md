# What the Work Revealed

![Preserved failures becoming durable tests, contracts, and stronger architecture](assets/discoveries-engineering-lessons.png)

## Preservation before intelligence

The work began with a practical inversion: an artifact’s existence has value even
when its usefulness, motive, truth, or relationship is unknown. Derived intelligence
must remain an overlay. A system that preserves only what its current model recognizes
as useful cannot later reconstruct what the model discarded.

## Time is part of meaning

Admission time, creation time, referred time, real-world inception, observation time,
validity time, and supersession time are different. Later context often makes earlier
material calculable, which is why discovery commonly walks from newer context toward
older evidence. Forward explanation must then replay the same selected graph rather
than invent a new story.

## Similarity proposes; it does not explain

Embeddings and lexical overlap are effective ways to find candidates. They do not
establish identity, cause, support, conflict, importance, or personal meaning. Typed
relations, exact source spans, temporal state, and uncertainty must survive beyond
the vector search.

## Empty reasoning is valid

Some artifacts contain no accepted reasoning relation. Some analyses attempt many
classifications and accept none. Preserving the attempted operands, scores, and empty
authoritative graph is more honest and more useful than fabricating a chain.

## One large item can defeat an otherwise bounded pipeline

Grouping documents into bounded packets did not help when one evidence item itself
was oversized. Deterministic intra-item slicing and restoration of original offsets
were required. Later stages also needed slice-level identities so composition and
selection could be independently proven.

## Provenance must remain source-bound

Selecting the first occurrence of a repeated quote creates false evidence. Ambiguous
source binding must fail or retain multiple candidates until a later stage can resolve
them.

## Preprocessing order changes the meaning of the pipeline

Discourse and coreference must run once over exact bounded evidence before semantic
relation extraction. Calling discourse independently from multiple stages duplicated
expensive work and made it impossible to prove that downstream relations used the
same measured structure.

## Learned scores are bounded observations

Purpose-trained factuality models were useful observers but failed to separate some
project-shaped reversals and status mutations. Agreement between models did not repair
that boundary. Exact structured facts moved to typed and hash-bound proofs; learned
scores remained bounded observations.

## Capability names can launder placeholders

A wrapper or class name can claim to be a specialist while executing a generic prompt
or deterministic projection. Runtime evidence must bind the exact implementation,
model or method identity, call edge, input and output hashes, and authority class.

## Reasoning and writing are separate jobs

An argument graph can be structurally correct while the document is unreadable. A
surface realizer can be fluent while dropping the relation that made two facts belong
together. The assembly line therefore separates evidence selection, relation
classification, product planning, microplanning, realization, product-aware quality,
and independent promotion.

A narrow prose advantage is not an overall system advantage. Blind frontier-agent
controls sometimes produced smoother paragraph transitions, while the local line
preserved and independently proved more of the sealed payload. Surface realization
is a bounded station that can be refined; provenance, temporal continuity, replayable
relations, and local operational independence are architectural capabilities. A
comparison must weight the requested product objective rather than declaring the
winner from sentence polish alone.

Resource scarcity can be a productive design constraint. The absence of a large
accelerator budget kept fluent general-model substitution distinct from
architecture. It forced durable external state, specialist responsibilities, leased
resources, replayable handoffs, and independent acceptance. Those mechanisms are now
strengths of the system rather than temporary accommodations. Additional compute may
accelerate a justified cog, but it must not erase the discipline that made the system
possible.

## Packets are not context

Selected source fragments alone produced disconnected or mechanical documents. A
portable working model also needs relations, chronology, epistemic state, rhetorical
jobs, receiver assumptions, expansion handles, ownership, and handoff history. That
“glue” is not filler; it is the information needed to reconstruct why the selected
material belongs together.

## Collapse is a graph operation

The reverse walk must see the provisional tree before pruning. Collapse is explicitly
lossy and product-specific, but it must preserve branch relationships, failures,
alternatives, uncertainty, and balance. Source volume measures cost, not automatic
importance. Interlaced refinement works toward the target product rather than making
one irreversible compression pass.

## Product structure belongs to the withdrawal

Genre, audience, length, rhetorical moves, and weave do not belong on source records.
They define one output. Different products may require different specialist order,
multiple instances of one capability, or refusal of a mechanism that another product
welcomes.

## Weave is a measured service

Literary and communication measurements revealed useful trajectories, packet sizes,
given/new balance, fragmentation, and product variation. They did not justify one
permanent configuration of ratios. The useful product is a versioned calibration
service that presents measured profiles, uncertainty, scaling curves, rights, and
applicability to line managers.

## Human compatibility is multi-axis

Grounding, readability, coherence, audience fit, attention budget, typography,
duplication, rhetorical completion, and weave can fail independently. One quality
score hides repair information. A product-aware manager must report the axes and ask
the responsible cog for a bounded correction.

## Resource cost belongs in the handoff

The heaviest stages were not always the ones adding the most value. Cold model loads,
duplicate discourse calls, unbounded evidence, verifier fan-out, and repeated retries
could dominate runtime. Batching, leases, backpressure, cancellation, checkpointing,
and query-plan-style contribution accounting make the assembly line both cheaper and
more inspectable.

## Public documentation is itself a product

A privacy filter cannot turn a private archive into a useful public explanation.
Public work requires its own architecture: coherent organization, sovereignty,
rights, attribution, generic transformation of private observations, do-no-harm
review, cadence abstraction, and exact snapshot identity. A useful public record can
preserve meaning and sovereignty together.

## External capability does not require corpus transfer

The frontier comparison showed that a prepared payload can support outside
realization without relocating the maintained corpus. The external model improved
surface form, but the relationships, provenance, temporal state, and machinery needed
for the next withdrawal remained local. The payload had enough information to be
useful and too little to reconstruct the durable system. That asymmetry does not
depend on provider memory or account preferences; it follows from what the payload
does and does not contain. The useful transaction therefore occurred without destroying
the human record or transferring its durable value to the receiving institution.
