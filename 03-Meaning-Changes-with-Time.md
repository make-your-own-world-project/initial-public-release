# Meaning Changes With Time

![Earlier evidence, later meaning, and changing interpretations remaining connected across time](assets/temporal-meaning-preserved-history.png)

## The map sits above the corpus

The corpus preserves deposits. A separate temporal layer records relationships,
interpretations, classification attempts, importance contributions, and their
history. That layer can change continuously without changing the original artifacts.

An earlier link is not deleted when a later interpretation is better. The later
observation can confirm, narrow, contradict, reclassify, or supersede it. The earlier
link remains valid as the view that was available at that state and may explain the
path to later work.

## Time is not one timestamp

The system distinguishes at least:

- when an artifact was observed by the corpus;
- when a particular representation was captured or created;
- when the real-world activity may have begun;
- when an event appears to have occurred;
- whether the artifact refers to past, present, future, mixed, timeless, or unknown
  time;
- when an interpretation was considered valid;
- when that interpretation was recorded;
- when a later observation superseded it.

A document admitted recently may concern work begun much earlier. A present note may
be recollection, prediction, worry, planning, or a timeless reflection. Admission is
never treated as proof of inception.

## Reasons are observations, not permanent labels

A reason-like relationship records its authority and epistemic status. It may be a
direct source statement, directly observed structure, specialist-derived candidate,
deterministic projection, supported inference, possible association, contradiction,
reattribution, or unknown.

Structural prominence, model confidence, repetition, centrality, and audience fit can
be useful dimensions. None is silently converted into what a person cared about.

## The Personal Meaning Matrix

The Personal Meaning Matrix is a request-scoped view over these source-bound
observations. It can hold multiple dimensions without forcing them into one universal
importance number. Candidate dimensions include:

- attention and effort investment;
- project and activity structure;
- discourse prominence and causal reach;
- evidence strength and uncertainty;
- conflict, correction, and open-question protection;
- external constraints and environmental conditions;
- values, identity, appraisal, and intended outcomes when evidenced;
- expressive mode, reflection, humor, irony, or artistic framing;
- persistence and change across time;
- relevance to a declared receiver and product.

The matrix is not a personality simulator and does not answer a timeless question
about what a person would say. Observer state is time-bound. A later state may differ
from an earlier one, and both remain part of the map.

## Backward discovery and forward reconstruction

Arbitrary data often becomes more intelligible when traversal begins from a later,
context-rich observation and moves toward earlier, sparser records. The reverse walk
selects exact deposits, relationships, chronology, and supersession history. It does
not choose final chapters or prune toward a predetermined thesis.

Forward reconstruction reverses traversal over the same selected graph. It does not
ask another model to invent a substitute story. Literary and Human Protocol
mechanisms may shape pacing, balance, and explanation, but the selected edge
identities remain the basis of the journey.

The withdrawal may stop at a historical boundary, use nonsequential source parts, or
produce unequal pages or volumes. Retrieval order, chronology, graph order, and
delivery order remain distinct.

## Honest empty states

A photograph, note, or task can remain an isolated deposit with no accepted semantic
relation. A reasoning analysis can produce zero authoritative links while preserving
its attempts and operands. An unsolved problem can remain unsolved. These are valid
states, not failures that require fabricated connective prose.

The exported working model is likewise a time-bound projection, not the temporal
record itself. An outside specialist may render the supplied state, but it does not
acquire the supersession history, evolving relationships, or authority needed to
define the next state.
